## Maintaining this ledger

The sections above appear in the game. Update creator, source, licence and use
when introducing resources. The notes below are for maintainers.

All models, environment art and procedural texture data are original Specimen
work. The adapted `hash13` lattice hash is credited above; dissolve interpolation,
corrosion, shader integration and matching shadow passes were written for this
project. Dependency versions are recorded in `package.json` and its lockfile.

Sound effects were supplied by the requester from ElevenLabs and are credited
under its free-plan terms: non-commercial use with attribution. Original packs live in
`assets/audio/`; exact archive/member hashes, edits and gains are retained in
`assets/audio/manifests/`. The turret recording was supplied separately and
transcoded to OGG. Importers are in `scripts/audio/`; see the
[audio guide](docs/architecture/gameplay-audio.md).

### Compiled cinematic music

First Sunlight uses the requester-supplied `first-sunlight-complete-ending.ogg`,
copied byte for byte to `public/assets/audio/music/blackout-first-sunlight-ending.ogg`.
The 179-second stereo OGG Vorbis combines Claimed by the Void at 0–105s,
silence at 105–111s and Exploration Theme at 111–179s. The approved edits,
dialogue level shaping, transitions and fades are already in the file; the
game does not re-encode or alter them. Both compositions remain individually
credited above and are included in the main credits and final rolling credits.

The requester-supplied `specimen-cinematic-music-integration.zip` provides the
two final runtime recordings, copied unchanged into `public/assets/audio/music/`:
`blackout-elevator-quiet-ascent.ogg` (85.068 seconds) and
`blackout-research-the-specimen.ogg` (277 seconds, subsequently replaced
unchanged from `specimen-act3-inevitable-revision.zip`). Original edits and the
rebuild script remain in the original package; only the two compiled OGGs ship.
The revision's 96-second preview is for listening only and is not shipped.
The research suite uses Sci-Fi Textures at 0–66s, Article 160 Section 2 at
66–123.5s, silence at 123.5–134s, Inevitable at 134–230s and Derelict Spaceship
at 230–277s. Inevitable's Act III extends/repeats its intro using the supplied
1.4-second equal-power loop crossfade and +2.7 dB initial lift, tapering into
the approved Act IV level. The 195s seam is filled for continuity; subsequent
fusion and blast accents remain unshifted. Act IV starts near original 0:42.55;
Derelict Spaceship starts at original 2:14. No runtime edits or extra players
are applied. All five active creator/source/licence/edit credits (including the
separate elevator track) remain in the Music table. Perpetual Tension is no
longer used in the runtime score.
