# Issue #20 evidence

## Authored grey-box coverage

- Room 1 uses the specified compact containment dimensions and preserves the
  locked-door, sticky-perimeter-wall, vent-route visual puzzle.
- Room 2 is a substantially larger vertical calibration chamber with a safe
  impact-scaled landing rebound, four lower jump lessons, a bounce-to-sticky-
  wall catch, two upper jumps, and the final exit balcony.
- Sticky behaviour is authored through the existing surface registry rather
  than inferred from visual colour. Ordinary Room 2 floors use the slime's
  controller-owned impact rebound instead of a special bounce-pad surface.
- The Room 1 vent and Room 2 route were refined from a Blender blockout and
  converted back into named box colliders compatible with the collision world.
