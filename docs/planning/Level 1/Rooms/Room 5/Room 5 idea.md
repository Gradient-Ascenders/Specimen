# Room 5 — Level 1 Finale

## 1. Room Purpose

Room 5 is the **final room of Level 1** and serves two purposes:

1. Act as the final traversal challenge for **Bob**, testing the movement mechanics the player learned throughout Level 1.
2. Introduce **Goop**, the corrosive slime, and teach the player the basic slime-swapping mechanic before transitioning into Level 2.

The overall flow is:

> **Room 4 elevator → sticky climb → vent → Room 5 traversal → free Goop → learn swapping → use Goop → enter exit vent → Level 2**

---

# 2. Transition From Room 4

Once the Room 4 elevator reaches the top:

- Sticky tiles are visible on the side wall of the elevator shaft.
- Bob uses these sticky surfaces to climb away from the elevator.
- The sticky route leads toward an open maintenance vent.
- Bob enters the vent and travels through another short air duct.
- The duct eventually opens into Room 5.

The Room 5 vent exit is located:

- on the side wall;
- close to floor level;
- near one end of the room.

This means Bob enters Room 5 from a low position and immediately sees how large and vertical the final challenge is.

---

# 3. Initial Room Reveal

Room 5 should be one of the largest rooms in Level 1.

However, it should feel **more cramped and complicated** than the previous rooms because it contains much more environmental machinery and traversal geometry.

Possible room contents include:

- pipes;
- machinery;
- platforms;
- broken catwalks;
- support pillars;
- laser equipment;
- suspended structures;
- crates;
- sticky wall panels;
- containment equipment.

The player's starting area should still be relatively safe so they have time to look around.

From the entrance, the player should immediately be able to identify two important landmarks:

1. **Goop's containment box in the centre of the room.**
2. **The glass observation/control room overlooking the containment area.**

---

# 4. Goop's Containment

Goop is trapped inside a large reinforced metal containment box.

The box sits on a tall pedestal near the centre of the room.

Example:

```text
             OBSERVATION ROOM
          ┌─────────────────────┐
          │                     │
          │       LEVER         │
          │                     │
          └────── GLASS ────────┘


              ┌─────────────┐
              │   HAZARD    │
              │     ☣       │
              │             │
              │    GOOP     │
              │             │
              └─────────────┘
                  █████
                  █████
                 PEDESTAL
```

The containment box should visually look more dangerous and reinforced than Bob's original containment.

Possible details:

- hazard symbols;
- corrosion warnings;
- reinforced metal panels;
- bolts;
- warning stripes;
- damaged or stained metal;
- scientific equipment facing the container.

The player should understand that this is another specimen before reaching it.

---

# 5. Main Objective

The objective is:

> **Reach the observation room and pull the containment-release lever.**

The observation room is separated from the main chamber by thick glass.

There is **no normal entrance accessible from the main room**.

Instead, there is an opening above the observation window.

The player therefore has to climb high enough to bounce through this opening.

---

# 6. Main Traversal Route

Room 5 acts as the final test of Bob's Level 1 movement abilities.

The route should combine:

- normal jumping;
- bouncing;
- sticky walls;
- isolated sticky tiles;
- wall-to-wall transfers;
- vertical traversal;
- lasers;
- environmental platforms.

The room should be difficult, but it should not introduce another major Bob mechanic.

The player is using everything they already learned.

A basic progression could be:

```text
VENT ENTRY
    │
    ▼
LOWER MACHINERY
    │
    ▼
FIRST PLATFORMS
    │
    ▼
CENTRAL PEDESTAL
    │
    ▼
GOOP'S METAL CONTAINER
    │
    ▼
HIGHER PLATFORMS
    │
    ▼
STICKY WALL SECTION
    │
    ▼
LASER / JUMP SECTION
    │
    ▼
FINAL HIGH PLATFORM
    │
    ▼
BOUNCE INTO OBSERVATION ROOM
```

---

# 7. Lower Traversal Section

The first part of the room should be moderately difficult.

It can contain:

- machinery to jump between;
- low platforms;
- a few simple lasers;
- bounce opportunities;
- easy sticky surfaces.

The purpose is to move the player toward the central containment pedestal while gradually gaining height.

---

# 8. Central Containment Platform

The containment structure itself becomes part of the traversal route.

Bob reaches the pedestal and climbs onto or around it.

The player can then use the top of Goop's containment box as another platform.

This lets the central objective also become part of the level geometry.

From the containment box, the player can reach the upper half of the room.

---

# 9. Upper Sticky-Wall Section

The upper section should contain the hardest sticky traversal in Level 1.

Instead of entire walls being sticky, only specific marked tiles are.

Example:

```text
WALL A                          WALL B

│  ███                            ███ │
│  ███                                │
│                                     │
│             ●                       │
│                                     │
│                         ███         │
│                         ███         │
│                                     │
│       ███                           │
│       ███                           │
```

The player must:

1. land on one sticky panel;
2. prepare their next jump;
3. bounce across the gap;
4. land on another sticky panel;
5. continue upward.

Some later transfers can include timed lasers.

The lasers should make the jumps harder without turning the section into another Room 4-style laser survival sequence.

---

# 10. Final High Platform

Completing the upper traversal leads Bob to the highest useful platform in the room.

From here, the opening above the observation-room window is reachable.

The player performs one final large bounce.

```text
           OBSERVATION ROOM

        ┌─────────────────┐
        │     OPENING     │
        └────────▲────────┘
                 │
                 │
               ● │
              /  │
             /   │
        █████████████
        HIGH PLATFORM
```

The opening should be generous enough that this jump feels satisfying rather than frustrating.

---

# 11. Observation Room

Once Bob enters the observation room, the difficult traversal stops.

This is a small safe area.

Inside:

- the containment chamber can be seen through the glass;
- Goop's box is visible below;
- scientific equipment surrounds the window;
- the containment-release lever is clearly visible.

There are no lasers or major platforming challenges here.

The player's only objective is to activate the lever.

---

# 12. Lever Interaction

The lever should use Bob's sticky ability rather than a normal interaction button.

Bob jumps onto the lever handle and sticks to it.

His weight pulls the lever downward.

```text
BEFORE

     │
     │
     │
     ●


AFTER

     \
      \
       ●
```

Once the lever reaches its fully lowered position:

> **The containment release begins.**

---

# 13. Goop Release Cutscene

Pulling the lever triggers a short in-engine cutscene.

Player control is temporarily removed.

The camera shows:

1. warning lights activating around the containment pedestal;
2. locking mechanisms disengaging;
3. the reinforced metal container opening;
4. Goop being revealed;
5. Goop jumping down from the containment pedestal onto the room floor.

The cutscene should be short.

Approximately:

> **5–8 seconds**

At the end, the camera returns to normal gameplay view.

---

# 14. Slime Swapping Introduction

Immediately after control returns, the player is shown a large temporary control prompt.

Example:

```text
SLIME SWAPPING

Press [KEY] to switch slime
```

Use whichever keybind is ultimately chosen by the control system.

The player can now swap between:

- **Bob** — sticky and bouncy;
- **Goop** — corrosive.

This is the first time in the game where multiple controllable slimes are available.

The prompt should disappear once the player successfully swaps for the first time.

---

# 15. First Goop Interaction

Room 5 should contain one very simple obstacle specifically for Goop.

Near the level exit is another ventilation opening.

However, the vent is blocked by a wooden/corrosible barricade or panel.

Example:

```text
              EXIT VENT

          ┌─────────────┐
          │   WOODEN    │
          │    PANEL    │
          │    █████    │
          └─────────────┘
```

Bob cannot remove it.

When controlling Goop, approaching or contacting the panel causes Goop's corrosive ability to dissolve through it.

The barricade should be visually marked as something Goop can destroy so the player understands the mechanic.

---

# 16. Goop Tutorial

This interaction should be intentionally simple.

The player only needs to learn:

> **Goop can dissolve marked obstacles that Bob cannot.**

There should not be another large puzzle after freeing Goop.

The sequence is simply:

```text
FREE GOOP
    │
    ▼
LEARN SLIME SWAPPING
    │
    ▼
SWITCH TO GOOP
    │
    ▼
APPROACH BLOCKED VENT
    │
    ▼
DISSOLVE WOODEN / CORROSIBLE PANEL
    │
    ▼
VENT OPENS
```

Level 2 can teach more complicated Goop puzzles later.

---

# 17. Level Exit

Once Goop destroys the vent barricade, the vent becomes accessible.

The player can enter it using either slime.

The game does not need to require both slimes to physically crawl into the vent separately.

Entering the vent with either active slime can trigger the Level 1 completion sequence.

The game then treats both slimes as travelling together.

---

# 18. Level 1 Ending Cutscene

Entering the final vent triggers a short transition cutscene.

One possible sequence:

1. active slime enters the vent;
2. the other slime follows;
3. both move deeper into the ventilation system;
4. the duct suddenly gives way or slopes downward;
5. Bob and Goop fall through the shaft together;
6. camera follows them falling;
7. screen transitions into Level 2.

Example:

```text
             LEVEL 1 VENT
                  │
          Bob →   │   ← Goop
                  │
                  ▼
             AIR DUCT
                  │
                  │
               BREAK /
                DROP
                  │
                  ▼
                 ● ●
                Bob
                Goop
                  │
                  ▼
          LEVEL 2 START
```

This creates a direct physical transition between the two levels rather than simply showing a loading screen.

---

# 19. Level 2 Arrival

Level 2 begins with both slimes arriving together.

The exact landing location can be designed with Level 2 later.

The important continuity is:

> Bob rescued Goop, both escaped through the vent, and they now enter the next part of the facility together.

From this point onward, Level 2 can properly teach:

- slime swapping;
- positioning inactive slimes;
- Goop's corrosive ability;
- puzzles requiring both slimes.

# 21. Scope Limits

For the basic version, Room 5 does **not** need:

- complicated Goop puzzles;
- multiple corrosible obstacles;
- advanced slime cooperation;
- pressure plates;
- both slimes independently reaching the exit;
- complex cutscene animation;
- new Bob abilities.

The critical Room 5 features are:

1. challenging final Bob traversal;
2. central Goop containment;
3. observation-room objective;
4. sticky lever interaction;
5. Goop release cutscene;
6. slime-swapping introduction;
7. one simple Goop corrosion tutorial;
8. vent transition into Level 2.

---

# 22. Room Identity

The one-sentence identity for Room 5 should be:

> **The final test of Bob's traversal abilities becomes the rescue and first introduction of Goop.**

The player finishes Level 1 having mastered Bob, then immediately receives a glimpse of how the game will expand once a second slime joins them.
