# Bob's opening trailer shot

Open the game with `?trailer=opening` (for example, `http://localhost:5173/?trailer=opening`).
This is a capture-only in-engine scene, not an automatic gameplay cutscene.
It reuses the Room 1 containment machine, egg materials and Bob's shared model and materials.
Normal game startup and gameplay remain unchanged.

The preparation screen links to [Goop's second shot](trailer-goop.md), available
at `?trailer=goop`. Each cutscene is independently replayable for editing.

Press **Play opening** after preparation finishes. Recording controls disappear.

- **Space** pauses/resumes.
- **R** replays from the closed door.
- **H** shows/hides controls, including the shot's timeline slider.
- Switching to another tab pauses playback.

The 20.5-second sequence establishes the laboratory door, opens it, moves into
the dark room with intermittent on/off lighting, switches on the main lights,
holds on the contained egg, then shows three crack stages and Bob hatching.
The visible fractures are the actual borders of
six irregular shell pieces. Cracking lasts about 4.6 seconds before opening;
Bob is already inside the opaque egg and is revealed smoothly as the pieces
slide outward and roll aside, leaving clearance for his gel deformation.
He settles onto the chamber floor and gently unfurls instead of appearing
after the shell opens. There are no separate fluid spurts, droplets or puddles.
The final frame holds for editing into the remaining gameplay footage. It does not
shatter the containment machine or invent additional gameplay.

All later-visible meshes and their lighting programs are prepared before the
play button is enabled. Only Room 1 and its entrance are built, rather than
loading all three levels to capture one shot. Capture from the production build
at the intended footage resolution; this mode does not encode or upload video.
